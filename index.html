What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a widely used screening tool that uses your height and weight to estimate body fatness. It's a quick and simple way to check if your weight is in a healthy range for your height. While BMI doesn't directly measure body fat, it correlates well with more direct measures for most people.

BMI Categories

  • Underweight — BMI below 18.5. May indicate insufficient nutrition.
  • Normal weight — BMI 18.5 to 24.9. Healthy weight range.
  • Overweight — BMI 25 to 29.9. Increased health risks.
  • Obese — BMI 30 or above. Significantly increased health risks.

BMI Formula

Metric: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

Imperial: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)²

Limitations of BMI

BMI is a useful screening tool but has limitations. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, overall body composition, or fat distribution. Athletes with high muscle mass may have a high BMI but low body fat. Always consult a healthcare professional for personalized health advice.

Healthy Weight Tips

  • Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains
  • Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week
  • Stay hydrated and limit sugary drinks
  • Get adequate sleep (7-9 hours for adults)
  • Consult your doctor before starting any weight loss program
